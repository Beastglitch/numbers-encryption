Text Encryptor

Encryption/Decryption Rules:
  • Letters to Numbers: Each letter is converted to its position in the alphabet (A=1, B=2, ..., Z=26)
  • Numbers to Letters: Each digit is converted to a letter (0=A, 1=B, 2=C, ..., 9=J)
  • Spaces and punctuation remain unchanged
  • Decryption: Reverses the process - converts numbers back to letters and letters back to numbers
